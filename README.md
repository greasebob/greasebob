### Hey, I'm on Discord and Roblox as BobGrease.

MIDI tools for Roblox and piano.

- **[QuartzMIDI](https://github.com/greasebob/QuartzMIDI)**: a Roblox piano auto player that plays MIDI files and your MIDI keyboard on any game piano.
- **[P2Piano](https://greasebob.github.io/p2piano/)**: an online piano room to play together from a link, with velocity and triple pedals.

Both are on **[greasebob.github.io](https://greasebob.github.io/)**.
